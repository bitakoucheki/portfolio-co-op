01 · About Me

Who I am.

I'm a Computer Science student at York University with a focus that has steadily sharpened toward one thing: building dependable systems out of data. What started as coursework in databases and algorithms has become hands-on work with production-grade enterprise data platforms.

Right now I'm on a data engineering co-op placement at Meridian Credit Union, working with the Enterprise Analytics & Data team. My day-to-day involves Microsoft Fabric, SQL, and data pipeline work — helping structure and govern data that drives analytics across the organization. Being placed on a project close to going into production, and collaborating with teams across business banking, wealth, and marketing, has given me a practical view of how data actually moves through an organization.

Before this, I spent time in AI quality and evaluation — training, prompting, and stress-testing large language models and agent workflows. That work taught me to think adversarially: find edge cases, surface failure modes, and care deeply about correctness. I carry that same rigor into data.

Outside of technical work, I have a classical music background and a long-running interest in music production. I built Motif, a browser-based generative music engine, as a way to treat composition like code. That blend of analytical thinking and creative instinct is something I bring to most things I do.
